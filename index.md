## [Projects](/projects/)

### ReservoirPy: Lightweight Reservoir Numerical Simulator
*2026/03 - Present*

A modular, extensible reservoir numerical simulator supporting single-phase and two-phase (IMPES) flow with ControlNet-style condition injection, CFL adaptive time-stepping, and geostatistical permeability field generation.

### FlowFormer: U-Net + Temporal Transformer for Reservoir Flow Prediction
*2026/04 - Present*

Deep learning surrogate model combining U-Net spatial modeling with Temporal Transformer for long-term reservoir flow field prediction, with dual decoder architecture for pressure and saturation.

### Adaptive Diffusion Research: Adaptive Time Sampling for Diffusion Models
*2026/03 - Present*

Researching adaptive sampling strategies for diffusion models to accelerate generation while maintaining quality.

### MindWeave for Obsidian: AI Knowledge Weaving Engine
*2026/04*

An Obsidian plugin that transforms scattered notes into structured Wiki knowledge bases using AI. Released v1.0.0.

### MultiDoc2Train: Multi-modal Training Data Pipeline
*2026/04*

A data processing pipeline converting HTML, PDF, and Markdown files to ModelScope JSONL training format with quality filtering and deduplication.

### Diffusion Model Implementation
*2026/03*

Implemented DDPM, Improved DDPM, and Score SDE diffusion models from scratch on MNIST and CIFAR-10.
